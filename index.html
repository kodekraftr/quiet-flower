The Song of Quiet Flower

Torn from a sunlit garden and chained in a mountain quarry, you piece together your own torn song and find it is a spell. Write melodies, cast them on whoever is listening, and sing your way out.

How to play

Sit down, put the controllers away and use your hands. Everything is within arm's reach.

Clap
Summon the staff, then pull your hands about 30 cm apart. Clap again to dismiss it.
Pinch
Look at something and pinch to pick it up. On the staff, pinch a coloured note from underneath and drop it on a line.
Look
Look at what you want the spell to reach. It glows when it's targeted.
Fist
Close a hand and hold until the gold ring fills to cast your melody.

No headset? Play with the keyboard

Drag or arrow keys to look · P pinch · Space staff · 1–4 notes · Backspace remove · Enter cast · Esc dismiss
